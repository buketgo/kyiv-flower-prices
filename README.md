# Kyiv flower prices (open data)

Open research bands for **flower / bouquet prices in Kyiv (2026)**.

- **Human article:** https://buketgo.com.ua/blog/tsiny-na-kvity-kyiv-2026  
- **Machine-readable:** [`prices-2026-09.json`](./prices-2026-09.json)  
- **License:** [MIT](./LICENSE) — free to use; keep copyright notice.

## Why this exists

Shop homepages dominate “ціни на квіти Київ”, but buyers need **transparent stem-count bands** and caveats (photo bait, delivery slots). This dataset is the structured twin of our public guide.
